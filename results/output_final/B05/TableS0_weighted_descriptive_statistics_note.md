# Table S0 weighted descriptive-statistics note

All point estimates use the final TALIS teacher weight (`TCHWGT`) in the primary available-item sample: valid recoded exposure and outcome, a final teacher weight, and at least one valid TT4G35A-C item. Each TT4G35 item row further restricts to a valid response coded 1-4 for that item; source codes 5, 8, and 9 are excluded from that item's summary. The composite is the mean of the observed valid TT4G35A-C items and therefore is not restricted to three-item complete cases.

For each row, the weighted mean is the `TCHWGT`-weighted mean. The weighted SD is the square root of the weighted second central moment using the population denominator. Weighted skewness is the weighted third central moment divided by SD cubed. `weighted_excess_kurtosis` is the weighted fourth central moment divided by SD to the fourth power, minus 3 (Pearson excess kurtosis). These are descriptive point estimates only; no confidence interval is reported.
