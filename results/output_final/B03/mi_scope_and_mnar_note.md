# B03 Multiple imputation and MNAR note

Twenty predictive-mean-matching chained-equation imputations are generated for the three perceived-utility items. Paths involving the imputed mediator are analysed with all 100 Fay-BRR replicate weights within each completed dataset. The public MI table combines mean within-imputation Fay-BRR variance and between-imputation variance by Rubin's rules. The c path is unaffected by the imputed mediator and has a direct Fay-BRR interval.

The MNAR delta scenarios replace missing utility items by each respondent's observed-item mean minus or plus 0.5, bounded to 1-4. They are transparent point-estimate sensitivity scenarios, not estimates of an identified missingness mechanism; no confidence interval is reported.
